## Bucket C - established branding agencies
- **Pentagram** - work-first. Plain grid of projects, tiny captions, and a sentence used as the filter
  ("We design [Everything] for [Everyone]") - the filter *is* copy. GSAP + Barba page transitions.
- **Koto** - full-bleed film hero, live local clock in the nav, one large statement paragraph, sticky
  "Our work" label beside stacked case-study cards. Calm, dark, cinematic.
- **Collins** - one centred serif line ("Rewrite your worth."), awards as tiny bracketed tags, then
  video, then a horizontal rail of case-study cards. Restraint carries the premium feel.
- **DixonBaxi** - "Ask us anything." prompt as the hero and an endless horizontal rail of work cards
  under it.
- **Porto Rocha** - dashboard: live clock + city, collapsible project list, news cards. Dense but ordered.
- **Mother Design** - toolbar navigation like a desktop OS, giant name, horizontal strip of floating
  images, split-word sections ("Depth / Care") with a *sticky note* card on top - scrapbook-adjacent.
- **Wolff Olins** - full-bleed video hero, serif statement with underlined key words, case cards with
  tag pills.
- **Base Design** - Feed / Projects / Index toggle; feed is an archive mosaic. Proof that a studio can
  lead with volume of work.
- **Studio Dumbar** - full-screen 3D/motion pieces, one per viewport. Lenis + GSAP ScrollTrigger.
- **Made Thought** - giant serif wordmark on white; long intro.
- **Lundgren+Lindqvist** - dense image grid, typewriter/mono captions.
- **Snask** - loud and playful; pink sections; personality in copy ("SNASK OFF!").

- **Here Design** - huge Baskerville "Here." then serif line with italic emphasis
  ("We design *brand ecosystems* with a *cultural purpose*."). Editorial calm; closest tonal match
  among the big agencies.
- **Pearlfisher** - illustrated hero, grey-to-white statement reveal on scroll, case cards with tags.
- **JKR** - statement paragraphs that light up line by line as you scroll (opacity scrub).
- **Gretel** - tiny type, big images, asymmetrical editorial grid, Squarespace.
