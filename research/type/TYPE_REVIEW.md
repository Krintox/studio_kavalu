# Type review (queue item Q6), 2026-10-24

**The question.** The client's second brief: "keep the fonts good. either use the reference and see
what sort of fonts look best for work like this." The wordmark had already been rebuilt from her
own lettering (Q3). This review covers every other face on the site.

**The answer.** Her lettering stays the wordmark. Around it, **Gambetta** (display serif, Indian Type
Foundry), **Switzer** (text grotesk, Indian Type Foundry) and **Courier Prime** (typewriter labels).
**Homemade Apple** (hand notes), **Mrs Saint Delafield** (her signature) and **Anek Kannada** (Kannada)
are kept. Fraunces, Ranade and IBM Plex Mono leave the site.

The references speak in grotesk, typewriter, handwriting and tracked labels. A display serif appears
only as small italic captions (1). None of them looks like a soft, wonky serif.

### 1.2 Her current site (`current_website/`, 4 screens)

Heavy extended caps (the STUDIO / KAVALU wordmark and "SERVICES"), a sturdy bracketed serif for
headings and the service cards ("Meet The Founder!", "Visual Identity"), a Helvetica-style body and
a script signature. So a serif for headings is continuity, but no particular serif is.

- **Grotesk is the professional default.** The families behind it are Suisse Intl, Neue Montreal,
  Neue Haas, Akkurat, ABC Diatype, Lausanne, GT America, Aeonik and Founders Grotesk.
- **About a third pair it with a serif**, and like-minded craft and lifestyle brands pair most often
  (41%). The serifs they choose are editorial and classic: Baskerville, Garamond, Editorial New,
  Freight, Kinfolk Serif, Saol, GT Sectra, Plantin, Caslon, Martina Plantijn, Copernicus.
- **Fraunces appears on 1 site of 226.** Instrument Serif and DM Serif, the other faces generated
  sites lean on, appear 2–3 times each.
- **Mono labels** are a studio habit (13%; 20% of motion-led studios), and two sites use typewriter
  faces (GT Alpina Typewriter, Cutive Mono).
- **Handwriting is rare (4%).** That makes it distinctive for Kavalu, and a reason to keep it to notes.

| Erode (ITF) | Neutral and light; a workhorse, not a voice. |
| Sentient (ITF) | Warm and friendly, but close to Fraunces in feel. |
| Zodiak (ITF) | High-contrast and fashion-leaning; beautiful, but closer to luxury than to a scrapbook. |
| Boska (ITF) | Very high contrast; at light weights the hairlines vanish on plum. |
| Bespoke Serif (ITF) | Competent, less distinctive. |
| Newsreader | Journalistic. |

| Face | Verdict |

## 2. The system

| Role | Face | Source / licence | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wordmark | Her own lettering (SVG) | Hers | `components/brand/Wordmark.tsx`; never typeset |
| Display | **Gambetta** variable 300–700, roman + italic | Fontshare (ITF), FFL 2.0, self-hosted | Headings at 300–400; italic for one phrase per heading |
| Text | **Switzer** variable 100–900, roman + italic | Fontshare (ITF), FFL 2.0, self-hosted | Body 400, UI 500 |
| Labels | **Courier Prime** 400 / 400 italic / 700 | Google Fonts, OFL 1.1, self-hosted | `size-adjust: 110%`: its caps are 0.58 em against Plex Mono's 0.70 |
| Notes | Homemade Apple | Google Fonts, OFL | Hand notes only, never UI |
| Signature | Mrs Saint Delafield | Google Fonts, OFL | "Ananya" only |
| Kannada | Anek Kannada (Ek Type) | Google Fonts, OFL | Also the ₹ fallback in every stack |

**The Indian thread.** Display and text come from Indian Type Foundry (Ahmedabad), the Kannada from
Ek Type (Mumbai), and the wordmark from her own hand. For a Bengaluru studio that story is true and
quietly useful.

**Gaps handled.**
- Gambetta, Switzer and Courier Prime lack the rupee sign. Anek Kannada now follows each Latin face in
  the stacks (`styles/tokens.css`), so "Under ₹1 lakh" in onboarding renders in an Indian sans and not
  in a stray system font.
- None of the faces (old or new) has →; arrows were and still are drawn by the system font.

**Licence notes** (`website/src/fonts/LICENSE-fonts.md`). FFL 2.0 permits commercial use and
self-hosting on the studio's own website. It forbids modifying the files (so no subsetting or
conversion), putting them in a public repository, or handing them to third parties. The studio
should download its own free copy from Fontshare.

## 3. Files changed

- `website/src/app/fonts.ts`: Gambetta, Switzer and Courier Prime (local); Fraunces, Ranade and Plex
  Mono removed.
- `website/src/fonts/`: `Gambetta-Variable*.woff2`, `Switzer-Variable*.woff2`, `CourierPrime-*.woff2`
  and `CourierPrime-OFL.txt` added; Ranade removed; `LICENSE-fonts.md` rewritten.
- `website/src/styles/tokens.css`: stacks now include the Kannada / ₹ fallback.
- 13 CSS modules: Fraunces-only `font-variation-settings` (SOFT, WONK, opsz) removed. The work-index
  hover keeps its `wght` 340 → 460 move, which Gambetta supports.
