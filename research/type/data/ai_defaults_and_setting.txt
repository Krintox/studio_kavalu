## PART 3. Authoritative references on setting type for screens

Line length (Butterick) — "The average line length should be 45–90 characters (including spaces)." — https://practicaltypography.com/summary-of-key-rules.html
Body size on the web (Butterick) — "In print, the most comfortable range for body text is 10–12 point. On the web, 15–25 pixels." — https://practicaltypography.com/typography-in-ten-minutes.html
Line height (Butterick) — "Line spacing should be 120–145% of the point size." — https://practicaltypography.com/summary-of-key-rules.html
Tracking for capitals (Butterick) — "That's why you always add 5–12% extra letterspacing to text in all caps or small caps." — https://practicaltypography.com/letterspacing.html
Tracking lowercase (Butterick) — "Lowercase letters don't ordinarily need letterspacing." — https://practicaltypography.com/letterspacing.html
System fonts (Butterick) — "ignore the fonts already loaded on your computer (known as system fonts)" / "never choose Times New Roman or Arial." — https://practicaltypography.com/typography-in-ten-minutes.html
How many typefaces (Butterick) — "Most documents can tolerate a second font. Few can tolerate a third. Almost none can tolerate four or more." — https://practicaltypography.com/mixing-fonts.html
Mixing rule (Butterick) — "Font mixing is most successful when each font has a consistent role in the document." — https://practicaltypography.com/mixing-fonts.html
Mixing method (Butterick) — "Mixing fonts is never a requirement—it's an option." — https://practicaltypography.com/mixing-fonts.html
Measure (Bringhurst via Rutter's Web Typography) — "Anything from 45 to 75 characters is widely regarded as a satisfactory length of line for a single-column page" — https://webtypography.net/2.1.2
Ideal measure (Bringhurst via Rutter) — "The 66-character line (counting both letters and spaces) is widely regarded as ideal." — https://webtypography.net/2.1.2

Line height (USWDS) — "Longer texts should have a line height of at least 1.5" — https://designsystem.digital.gov/components/typography/
